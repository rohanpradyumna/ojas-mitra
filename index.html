the maker

A promise kept in a courtyard.

Kavita Ajay began this from her own kitchen, in a small village in south India.

Kavita, ’26

Kavita Ajay began Ojas Mithra in the courtyard of her own home. At forty-six, she has spent more than half her life among families and farms across south India — a climate activist by training, a community psychologist by listening, learning what people put in their hands and their water, and what they wished they could put instead.

Her mother, before she passed, asked her to make something that would reach Indian homes at scale — quietly, kindly, without harm. This is that promise, in bottles.

Every order is filled from her backyard. The recipes use bio-enzymes — fruit waste fermented for three months in clay pots — and plant extracts gathered from the trees and gardens around her. Nothing leaves the kitchen until it has been on the shelf long enough that we, the family, have used it ourselves.

— with care, Kavita

Dishwash

Tough on grease. Gentle on the hands that wash up after every meal.बर्तन धोना

This is the bottle that sits beside the kitchen sink. It is what we use to wash the kadhai after the day's cooking, the steel plates after dinner, and the glass jars after the chutney has been finished.

The fragrance is lemon and a faint note of lemongrass. It is the smell of a kitchen being put to bed for the night.

what is inside
  • Bio-enzymes from citrus peel, jaggery and water, fermented for ninety days.
  • Saponified coconut oil with cold-pressed lemon extract.
  • Lemongrass, tulsi and aloe in a gentle plant-derived base.

Hand wash

Aloe and tulsi, in a quiet base. For hands that have been outside, or inside, or both.हाथ धोना

A hand wash that you can keep beside the kitchen sink and the bathroom basin without thinking twice. The aloe softens the skin; the tulsi steadies the cleaning; the base is plant-derived and biodegradable.

There is no synthetic fragrance, only the smell of the leaves themselves, and a Telugu-labelled version sits in the homes nearest ours.

what is inside
  • Bio-enzymes with aloe vera juice and tulsi leaf extract.
  • Saponified coconut oil, neem leaf infusion and plant glycerine.
  • A plant-derived foaming base that keeps the wash mild.

Floor cleaner

Neem, lemon, eucalyptus. The way a house smells just after the morning mop.फ़र्श साफ़ करना

The bottle that sits in the corner near the broom. A capful into a bucket of water is enough; the floors come up clean and the room takes on the smell of a temple courtyard at dawn.

It is safe on tiles, marble, granite, wood and the strange in-between surfaces that old Indian houses have. Pets walk on it. Children sit on it. We thought about that.

what is inside
  • Bio-enzyme citrus base with neem leaf extract.
  • Lemon and eucalyptus essential oils for the bright herbal lift.
  • Plant-derived surfactants with tulsi and fermented citric acid.

Vegetables & fruits

For what comes off the cart, the field, or someone’s hands. Soak, rinse, eat.फल और सब्ज़ी

Vegetables and fruits in India have travelled. They have been handled by many hands, sat on carts in the sun, and may have been sprayed once or twice.

This cleanser is for taking off what should not be on them, without leaving a taste of soap behind. Spray, soak for two minutes, rinse with water. That is all.

what is inside
  • Bio-enzyme citrus base with edible-grade plant surfactants.
  • Citric acid and cold-pressed lemon for a clean rinse.
  • A pared-back formula made to stay close to the kitchen sink.

Laundry liquid

Low foam, for buckets and machines alike. Colours remember themselves.कपड़े धोना

A laundry liquid for the way most Indian families actually do laundry: sometimes in a machine, sometimes by hand in a bucket, sometimes with a stone in the courtyard.

It is low-foam, so it rinses out cleanly however you wash. It does not strip colour from the cottons your mother gave you, and it leaves old cotton looking like old cotton.

what is inside
  • Bio-enzymes with saponified coconut and plant-derived stain enzymes.
  • Soapnut extract and soda ash for everyday wash loads.
  • Lavender with a citrus blend for a light clean finish.
