the shelf

Seven things, kept the way
we’d keep them at home.

Nothing here is in a range. There are no bestsellers. Each bottle exists because we use it in our own kitchen, on our own floors, on our own faces — and we wanted to make it well enough to share.

Dishwash i.

Tough on grease, gentle on the hands that wash up after every meal. Lemon and lemongrass.
Hand wash ii.

Aloe and tulsi, in a quiet base. For hands that have been outside, or inside, or both.
Toilet cleaner iii.

Thick enough to cling. Kind to septic tanks and to the people who clean the bathroom.
Floor cleaner iv.

Neem, lemon, eucalyptus. The way a house smells just after the morning mop.
Vegetables & fruits v.

For what comes off the cart, the field, or someone’s hands. Soak, rinse, eat.
Laundry liquid vi.

Low foam, for buckets and machines alike. Colours hold their own a little longer.
Kumkumadhi Tailam vii.

An old Ayurvedic oil for the face. Saffron, sandalwood, lotus, patience.
more, slowly —

A floor wipe, a body soap, a hair oil. Whichever is ready first. We will tell you here.
